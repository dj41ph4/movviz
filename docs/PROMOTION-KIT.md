# Movviz — Promotion & Press Kit

This file contains reusable, accurate copy for people who want to present Movviz in directories, communities, articles, videos or social posts.

## One-line pitch

**Movviz is an open-source, self-hosted all-in-one media platform combining discovery, requests, movie/TV automation, Torznab/Newznab search, a built-in BitTorrent engine, playback, recommendations, AI assistance and native Android TV/mobile clients.**

## Short pitch

Movviz replaces the usual chain of disconnected media tools with one coherent self-hosted application. It can discover and request movies/series, search Torznab/Newznab indexers, score releases, download through its integrated BitTorrent engine, import and rename files, maintain a library, upgrade quality automatically, play media directly/remux/transcode it, synchronize watched state and resume positions, recommend content and provide native Android TV/mobile clients. Plex integration is optional.

## Elevator pitch

Self-hosted media stacks are powerful, but often require a collection of applications for requests, movies, TV, indexers, downloading, playback and monitoring. Movviz approaches the problem as one product rather than a collection of dashboards. The same system understands what the user wants, what is missing, what is downloading, what is available, what has been watched and what the user may want next. It runs on Windows, Linux and Docker/NAS, includes native Android TV and Android mobile clients, and can interoperate with Plex without depending on it.

## Positioning

Movviz can be described as:

- an **all-in-one self-hosted media automation and playback platform**;
- a **single-app alternative to a traditional Seerr + Radarr + Sonarr + Prowlarr + torrent-client workflow**;
- a **personal streaming platform with acquisition automation built in**;
- a **Plex-compatible but Plex-optional media manager/player**.

Avoid describing Movviz as a Plex plugin. It is a standalone platform.

## Main differentiators

1. **One coherent product** — discovery, requests, acquisition, library, playback and recommendations share the same state and UX.
2. **Built-in BitTorrent engine** — no mandatory external download client.
3. **Built-in playback** — direct play, remux and transcoding with FFmpeg, hardware acceleration and HDR→SDR when required.
4. **Plex is optional** — Movviz can play local media directly while still supporting Plex synchronization and watch history.
5. **Native TV and mobile clients** — Kotlin / Jetpack Compose apps for Android TV and Android mobile.
6. **Real-time multi-device state** — watched state, resume positions and download/library changes are synchronized across clients.
7. **Personal recommendations** — history, ratings, likes/dislikes, actors, directors and genres contribute to suggestions.
8. **Conversational assistant** — optional AI can recommend, answer questions and perform actions such as adding or launching content.
9. **Operational tooling** — centralized diagnostics, health indicators, logs, jobs and transcoding benchmarks.
10. **Runs where self-hosters run** — Windows, Linux, Docker, x64 and ARM64.
